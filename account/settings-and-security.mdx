---
title: "Account settings and security"
description: "Update your profile, password, and account-deletion request safely."
---

Open **Settings** to see your signed-in identity, active plan, and regular/add-on credit balance.

## Profile

You can update your display name and profile image. Images must be PNG, JPG, or WebP and no larger than 1.5 MB. Email changes require Support verification.

## Password

Email/password accounts can change their password from **Settings**. The form requires the current password, a new password of at least eight characters, and confirmation. A successful change signs out all existing sessions.

Google-only accounts manage their password with Google. If you cannot sign in with Google, follow [Login and payment troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/login-and-payments).

## Delete account

The **Delete account** form schedules permanent deletion after a 30-day recovery window and signs you out immediately. You must confirm your email and type `DELETE`; password accounts must also confirm the current password.

<Warning>
  Permanent deletion ends all Blueprints and remaining regular or add-on credits. Contact Support during the recovery window if you need to cancel the request.
</Warning>

Account deletion may be held for an open payment, legal/operational hold, or unresolved Support case. The public [Retention Policy](https://www.aiblueprint.web.id/retention-policy) explains dormant-account notices and permanent deletion.
