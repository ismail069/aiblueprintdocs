## The two approval moments

1. **Idea Review / VPC Sign Approve** locks the Smart Requirement Summary and validated product context. This action automatically starts document generation.
2. **Per-document Sign Approve** records your acceptance of each current generated document. Blueprint Summary reaches **10/10 Approved** when every applicable current document is signed.

These are two approval types, not two global gates. There is no global Structure Review Package approval after generation.
