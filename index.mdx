## The two approval moments

1. **Idea Review / VPC Sign Approve** locks the Smart Requirement Summary and validated product context. This action automatically starts document generation.
2. **Sign Approve Blueprint** records your acceptance of the complete current document set. It becomes available after generation finishes, all 10 current document cards are **Reviewed**, and no repair or blocker prevents approval.

Opening a current document changes its review state from **Not yet reviewed** to **Reviewed**. This is not a signature. While generation is active, document cards show a spinner and **Project Health** shows **Generating**.

The **Project ZIP** remains locked until the current Blueprint has global approval. Regeneration, restore, or a new document version can require review and **Sign Approve Blueprint** again.
