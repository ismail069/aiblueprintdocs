One AI credit has a public value of **Rp1,000**. Your balance combines regular plan credits and add-on credits, but the Billing page shows both sources separately.

## Credit order

AI Blueprint always uses **regular plan credits first**. If the regular balance is empty or insufficient, it uses the required remainder from add-on credits.

| Action | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| Full Blueprint generation | 25 credits |
| Major cascade regeneration | 23 credits |
| Scoped single-document rewrite | 3 credits |
| Blueprint Assistant message | Minimum 0.2 credit; final charge follows accepted input/output cost and rounds up in 0.2-credit increments |
| Literal direct edit | Free |
| Resume a paid interrupted run | Free while the original paid entitlement remains valid |

Internal retries, fallbacks, and repairs are not separate customer actions and are not billed separately. A run that publishes nothing is refunded automatically.

Top-up credits have no time-based expiry while your account remains active. They end when the account is permanently deleted. Read the public [Retention Policy](https://www.aiblueprint.web.id/retention-policy) for the complete dormant-account and deletion terms.

## Midtrans transaction fee

Each checkout adds the applicable Midtrans transaction fee to the amount paid by the customer. The current Virtual Account or bank-transfer fee shown by AI Blueprint is **Rp4,000 per transaction**. The checkout total is authoritative for the selected payment method.

<Warning>
  Do not close a payment tab until Midtrans shows a final state. If payment succeeds but credits do not appear after a reasonable refresh, keep the order reference and open Support. Do not pay the same order twice.
</Warning>

## Payment and credit history

**Billing** shows successful payments, invoice references, credit grants, top-ups, debits, and refunds. You can filter credit history by date and export it as CSV.

For cancellation and refund eligibility, read the public [Terms](https://www.aiblueprint.web.id/terms) and [Refund Policy](https://www.aiblueprint.web.id/refund-policy).
